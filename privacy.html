This website shows Elyrviel's own videos from TikTok, YouTube and Facebook, plus an MLBB skin event simulator. You do not need an account to use it, and the site does not ask you to log in.

What the site collects from visitors

  • No names, emails or passwords.
  • Simulator settings you choose are saved only in your own browser and can be cleared at any time.
  • The hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

TikTok data

The site uses TikTok's Login Kit and Display API only with Elyrviel's own TikTok account, to show Elyrviel's public videos and their view, like and comment counts. It does not access any visitor's TikTok account. The access token is stored encrypted on the server and is used for nothing else. Elyrviel can remove this access at any time in TikTok's settings.

Embedded videos

Videos play in TikTok's and YouTube's own players. When you play one, TikTok or Google may collect data under their own privacy policies. YouTube videos use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player.

Children

The site does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may ask what data is held about you and ask for it to be deleted. Contact Elyrviel through the social accounts linked on the site.
